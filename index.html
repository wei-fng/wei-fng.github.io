Welcome to my academic homepage.

I am Wei Feng (冯薇), a Ph.D. student at the School of Architecture, Southeast University, advised by Prof. Tang Peng. My research explores AI approaches to understanding built heritage. Drawing on graph-based deep learning and temporal knowledge representation, I investigate the spatio-temporal organization and transformation of historic built environments.

我是冯薇，东南大学建筑学院建筑运算与应用实验室（Lab. AAA）在读博士生， 师从唐芃教授。 研究聚焦人工智能辅助的建成遗产理解， 主要通过图深度学习与时序知识表征， 探索历史建成环境的时空组织与演变。
