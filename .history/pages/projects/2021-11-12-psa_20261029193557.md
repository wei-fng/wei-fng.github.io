Working as part of a student construction team from Southeast University, the project translated Hejduk's conceptual architectural drawings into a physical exhibition installation, providing hands-on experience in architectural interpretation, physical fabrication, and assembly.

参与根据海杜克“兰开斯特／汉诺威假面舞”系列作品《监狱》（Prison）进行的大尺度实体结构体制作与现场搭建。
项目将海杜克的概念性建筑图纸转化为实体展览装置，涉及建筑作品的空间与构造解读、实体制作及现场装配实践。
