This collaborative urban design project explores the regeneration of the former Jinling Shipyard waterfront district in Nanjing, China.

Focusing on **transportation planning**, the project combines land-use analysis, transport demand forecasting, road network assignment, and multi-modal mobility planning to evaluate the relationship between future urban development and accessibility. The proposed strategies seek to improve network connectivity, integrate public transportation, and establish a pedestrian-oriented mobility system linking the waterfront, neighborhoods, and major activity destinations.

The project demonstrates how **quantitative transport analysis can inform urban design decisions**, connecting development scenarios and network performance with spatial planning interventions.

本项目以南京金陵船厂及其周边滨江片区为研究对象，探索原有工业场地向复合型城市滨水空间的更新转型。基地位于长江与幕府山之间，规划面积约166公顷。设计关注原有工业场地封闭造成的城市交通联系不足，以及工业遗产、滨江景观与新兴城市功能之间的空间整合。

在**交通设计专项**中，综合运用土地利用分析、交通需求预测、路网交通分配与多模式交通规划等方法，评估不同开发情景下的交通需求及道路网络运行情况，并提出路网优化、公共交通整合与慢行系统组织等策略。

项目探索如何将**定量交通分析转化为城市设计依据**，通过交通组织与空间干预的协同，改善工业更新片区的可达性、公共空间联系及滨水步行体验。
