  <p>
    The workshop explored knowledge-driven approaches to
    heritage interpretation and generative design.
    Through ontology modelling, knowledge graphs,
    Graph RAG, and generative AI, participants investigated
    how structured historical knowledge could inform
    the understanding and creative reimagining
    of historic districts.
  </p>

  <p class="wf-workshop-chinese">
    本次工作营围绕历史街区的知识解码与人工智能重构，
    探索知识驱动的历史遗产解读与生成式设计方法。
    通过本体建模、知识图谱、Graph RAG 与生成式人工智能等技术，
    研究历史知识的结构化表达、关联与应用，
    并探索其在历史街区理解和创新性设计中的潜力。
  </p>
