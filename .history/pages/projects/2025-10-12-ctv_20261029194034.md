This independent research project developed a spatio-temporal building dataset covering **8,155 traditional villages across China**. The dataset aims to provide consistent and comparable building-level spatial data for investigating the morphology, spatial organization, and transformation of historic rural settlements. The data production workflow integrates **object detection, image segmentation, historical change detection, and multi-source geospatial data fusion**.

本项目独立构建了覆盖全国 **8,155 个中国传统村落**的建筑时空数据集，旨在为历史乡村聚落的建筑形态、空间组织及其演变研究提供具有一致性和可比性的建筑级空间数据。数据生产流程综合采用**目标检测、图像分割、历史变化检测与多源地理空间数据融合**等方法。
