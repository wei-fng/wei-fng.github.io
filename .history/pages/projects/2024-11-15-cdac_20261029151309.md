Participated in **Decoding Syntax**, a workshop held as part of the **2024 Computational Design Academic Conference (CDAC 2024)** in Shanghai, China. The workshop provided an opportunity to explore computational and generative approaches to architectural and urban design.

City Evolution-街道步行空间的自主更新

关注公共空间和建筑空间的演变，通过主动智能体（行人）与被动智能体（空间）的系统互动，分析城市空间与行人活动轨迹的关系，动态调整步行空间特性，最终提升步行空间活力，以反映城市空间的自我优化过程。
