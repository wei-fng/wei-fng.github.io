### Overview

This study investigates relational similarities among 8,155 Chinese traditional villages through spatio-temporal graph representations. By modelling the spatial organization of settlements and examining geographical, cultural, and industrial contexts, it explores how meaningful commonalities can be identified beyond visible morphological resemblance, offering a computational perspective on the comparative study of living historic settlements.
